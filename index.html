About me

Singapore-raised and Australia-educated, I started out in the Singapore Armed Forces’ Supply Command, then spent the next decade moving between corporate transformation, boutique consulting and startups — airlines, hospitality tech, construction, Web3, and most recently a $1B+ healthcare network, where I co-led an $18m finance and supply-chain transformation through to May 2026.

In 2024 I co-founded Golden Island Cruises with three local partners — a maritime tourism company in Lombok built on Blue Economy principles, now running a fleet of traditional Phinisi boats and six travel brands. I’ve also taught myself enough web development to ship a few small tools of my own. I like problems that need both a spreadsheet and a handshake — in English or Mandarin.
