The venture · Lombok, Indonesia

Golden Island Cruises

Co-founded in late 2024 with three local partners — a licensed, thirty-strong maritime tourism company sailing traditional Phinisi boats between Lombok and Komodo National Park, built on Blue Economy principles.

The signature voyage · 4 days, 3 nights

01 — Set sail from Lombok

Open water, hidden sandbars

The crossing to Labuan Bajo threads past empty white sandbars and reefs you can only reach by boat — four days of island-hopping in either direction.

02 — Komodo National Park

Walk with dragons

A guided trek through the UNESCO-listed park to meet the world's largest lizard where it actually lives — one of the seven wonders of nature.

03 — Padar Island

The ridge walk

A dawn climb over three sweeping bays — the view that made this corner of Indonesia famous.

04 — Beneath the surface

Mantas, turtles, Pink Beach

Snorkelling with manta rays and — in season — whale sharks, plus one of the world's few pink-sand beaches.

05 — Aboard the Phinisi

Built by Sulawesi shipwrights

Traditional boats in two classes, Superior and Deluxe, crewed by locals and held to international safety and comfort benchmarks.

Tourism that gives back

Boats built by traditional Sulawesi shipwrights, a locally hired crew, and a partnership-pathway equity scheme so staff share in the upside — commercially sound, community-first.

Run like a real company

Licensed by Indonesia’s Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries. Four co-founders, thirty people. I lead strategy, financial modelling and governance — from valuations and cashflow forecasting to dynamic seasonal pricing. Guests rate us warmly on Tripadvisor.
